# Music and Atmosphere

The original v1.21 score supports the **Full-Spectrum Blackout: Polar Night Link Loss** scenario. Music responds to scenario phases and combat intensity without affecting simulation mechanics.

## *Silent Meridian*

The in-scenario dynamic score is formed from several phase-oriented layers:

- **Polar-night patrol:** cold, spacious and restrained, accompanying the fleet as it follows its preplanned route beneath the aurora.
- **Data-link pulse:** a clear but increasingly unstable rhythm representing Link 11 polling, tactical-picture exchange and growing communication pressure.
- **Contact and intercept:** raises tension as tracks appear, CAP commits and fire-control decisions are made.
- **Missile engagement:** supports long-range weapon release, ship air defense and dense engagements as the available response window contracts.
- **Total-band denial:** becomes unsettled and fragmented when the shared picture collapses, expressing local action without a complete view of the battle.
- **Recovery:** communication returns without immediate relief, reflecting delayed reports, conflicting tracks and post-engagement uncertainty.

## *Across the Silent Band*

A five-minute scenario theme. Its opening conveys the scale and purpose of a fleet operating in the polar night. The music then moves into the pressure created by solar activity, degrading communications and an approaching air strike. A quicker pulse in the later section represents confusion and independent decisions during the blackout. The main theme returns near the end as the force begins to reconstruct its tactical picture, leaving a restrained sense of consequence and uncertainty.

The theme can be played from the Polar Night Link Loss briefing. The dynamic score changes automatically while the scenario is running.
