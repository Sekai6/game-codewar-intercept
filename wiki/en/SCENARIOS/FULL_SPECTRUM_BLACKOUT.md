# Full-Spectrum Blackout: Polar Link Loss

This 15–18 minute AI scenario models a solar-disturbance-driven degradation of HF, GNSS and tactical networks in the Norwegian Sea–Barents Sea. It uses progressive noise, delay, loss and deterministic communication windows rather than switching every sensor off.

The 0–150 s phase establishes the network; 150–300 s brings the search and escort groups into contact; 300–720 s is the blackout main battle and Tu-16K KSR-5 strike; 720–960 s provides recovery windows; 960–1080 s is final assessment. Observe local CG-57 defense, legal VLS launches, CAP decisions, A-6E anti-ship behavior and gradual track reconciliation.
