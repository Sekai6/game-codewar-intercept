# Verification Matrix

Build and scenario checks cover type safety and schema compilation. CEC checks cover measurement fusion and fire-control gating. Fleet/VLS checks prove entity launchers, ammunition and physical separation. Air checks cover Phoenix/Sparrow/IR seeker behavior. Datalink checks cover era gating and degradation. ACMI checks cover real entities, events and diagnostic properties.

Browser verification is single-renderer and serial with short fixed scenarios; long multi-seed runs are local-only.
