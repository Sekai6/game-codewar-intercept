# Telemetry and Tacview

The telemetry pipeline records measurements, tracks, datalink delivery, weapon decisions, legal launches, guidance phases, intercepts, damage, departures and mission outcomes. HUD, AAR, analytics and ACMI/Tacview consume the same event model.

ACMI exports real entity trajectories and adds CEC, EMCON, track quality, midcourse-link and communications-window properties without inventing weapon entities.
