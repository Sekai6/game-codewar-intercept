# Air Dynamics and AI

> v1.0.0 · 2026-07-26 | [Mechanics](README.md) | [中文](../../MECHANICS/AIR_DYNAMICS.md)

Aircraft integrate three-dimensional velocity using thrust, drag, lift/induced drag, mass, wing area, angle of attack, control response, engine spool, available load factor, roll/pitch rates, and fuel. Low-energy high-AoA flight can stall; high-speed turns remain structurally limited. Damage reduces thrust, control authority, sensors, or weapon availability.

Throttle regimes are idle, cruise, military, and afterburner where supported. Afterburner has separate acceleration/speed benefit, fuel multiplier, infrared multiplier, and time budget. AI selects power from mission, intercept geometry, launch zone, missile warning/TTI, energy, egress urgency, and fuel. Bombers and A-6E do not receive fighter afterburner behavior.
