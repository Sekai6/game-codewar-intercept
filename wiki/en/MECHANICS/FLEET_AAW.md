# Fleet Air Defense

> v1.0.0 · 2026-07-26 | [Mechanics](README.md) | [中文](../../MECHANICS/FLEET_AAW.md)

Every ship is an independent entity. Fleet command assigns OTC/AAWC roles, formation stations, threat sectors, and engagement tasks, but cannot create missiles, consume another ship's magazine, or bypass its launcher.

Link 11/16 contributes a cue-quality force picture with delay and uncertainty. The selected shooter must acquire/maintain its own valid track, accept the assignment, reserve channels and ammunition, and execute its Mk 10 or Mk 41 state machine. Physical launch evidence records ship, launcher, cell/rail, weapon, point, and time. Companion-ship fire is therefore verifiable in AAR and ACMI.
