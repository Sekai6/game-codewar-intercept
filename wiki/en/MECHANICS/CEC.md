# Cooperative Engagement Capability (CEC)

CEC is a measurement-level cooperative layer separate from Link 11/Link 16. It is available only in the explicit `cec-enabled` era for Long Beach, CG-57 and E-2C.

Platforms share timestamped measurements with velocity, position and covariance. Fusion degrades with age, synchronization and uncertainty. A composite track may support local fire-control calculation, but CEC never creates a missile, spends ammunition or bypasses a launcher.

Historical AIM-54A/C remains launch-platform/AWG-9 guided. Only the hypothetical `AIM-54X-CEC` receives network-native CEC midcourse updates, and only in the CEC era.
