# Datalink Eras

`ntu-baseline` provides Link 11; `jtids-transition` adds selected airborne JTIDS; `link16-modernized` provides the game-scaled Link 16 network; `cec-enabled` adds the separate measurement-level CEC layer.

Link 11/16 track reports and CEC measurements are not interchangeable. A cue never authorizes a weapon by itself. CEC still requires local fire control, channels, stores and the physical launcher path. Soviet platforms never register as CEC participants.
