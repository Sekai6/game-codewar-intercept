# AIM-54X CEC Phoenix (Hypothetical)

`AIM-54X-CEC` is a clearly marked future/game-only variant. It is not used by the historical AIM-54A/C entries or by NTU, JTIDS-transition or ordinary Link 16 eras.

In `cec-enabled`, the weapon may receive measurement-level midcourse updates from the CEC network. It still requires a legal launch solution, an available F-14 hardpoint and the normal terminal active-radar seeker acquisition. CEC does not bypass fire-control or launcher routines.
