# AIM-7F Sparrow

> v1.0.0 · 2026-07-26 | [Parameters](MISSILE_PARAMETERS.md) | [中文](../../WEAPONS/AIM7F_SPARROW.md)

A medium-range semi-active-radar weapon carried by F-14A. Game range is 5-460, speed 15, turn limit 24 deg/s, seeker range 36, damage 64, and countermeasure resistance 0.58.

The shooter must maintain illumination. Loss of the illumination channel breaks terminal support rather than silently converting the missile to active guidance. The weapon also obeys propulsion decay, FOV, chaff/ECM competition, target geometry, and proximity-fuze resolution.
